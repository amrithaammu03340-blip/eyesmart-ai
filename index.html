📏 Distance Visual Acuity

1 metre test
1. Stand or sit exactly 1.00 metre from the tablet screen.
2. Keep the screen approximately at eye level.
3. Cover one eye gently without pressing it.
4. Read the letters from top to bottom.
5. Test the other eye separately.
6. Keep browser zoom at 100%.

📖 Near Visual Acuity

40 cm test
1. Hold the tablet/chart exactly 40 cm from the eyes.
2. Keep the chart at a comfortable eye level.
3. Cover one eye gently without pressing.
4. Read the lines from top to bottom.
5. Repeat with the other eye.

🔲 Amsler Grid

1. Test one eye at a time.
2. Cover the other eye without pressing.
3. Keep the centre dot exactly in focus.
4. Report any missing, distorted, bent or wavy lines.
